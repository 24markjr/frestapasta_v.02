Phase 3 — Design the customer onboarding flow
Once your integration is implemented, the restaurant owner should experience the following:

1. Open OwnerOS → Settings → WhatsApp
The owner clicks Connect WhatsApp.




2. Authorize through Meta
The owner selects their business assets and authorizes access.




3. RestroOS completes setup
Your backend stores the tenant's account mapping, completes required registration and enables webhooks.




4. Test and activate
Verify the number and templates, then enable booking notifications.



Meta's onboarding process includes additional server-side steps beyond opening the signup popup, so implementing the popup alone is not sufficient.

Phase 4 — Decide who pays for WhatsApp messages
This is a business decision you need to make before launching:
- Restaurant-paid model: Each restaurant manages its own WhatsApp billing and payment method.
